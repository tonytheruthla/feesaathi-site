Free tools for tutors

Short answer

Small tools that run in your browser, need no sign-up, and store nothing. Built for Indian private tutors who want the job done in a minute rather than an account created.

Tools

Fee reminder message generator

Write a polite fee reminder in Hinglish or English, with your UPI link, and copy it into WhatsApp.

More are coming: a tuition fee receipt generator and a fee structure calculator. If one would save you an hour a month, write to rahul@feesaathi.com and say which.

Add your students, tap once, and every parent gets a WhatsApp reminder with your UPI link. Free for your first 10 students, no card.
