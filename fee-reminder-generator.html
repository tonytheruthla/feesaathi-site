Fee reminder message generator

Short answer

Fill in the student's name, the amount and the due date, pick Hinglish or English, and copy a polite fee reminder you can paste into WhatsApp. Add your UPI ID and it goes into the message. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent from this page.

How to use these messages

Pick the moment first, then the words. The before the due date message does most of the work, because it removes the only excuse that always lands, which is that the parent forgot. The offer a way out message is the one that gets a reply when the others have gone quiet, because it stops being a demand.

Three messages across a month is the ceiling. A fourth trains the parent to ignore you, and a phone call does more at that point than another line of text.

Doing this for every student, in one tap

Generating one message is easy. Generating fourteen of them, each with the right name, the right amount and a payment link that marks itself paid, is the part that eats a working day every month. That is the job FeeSaathi does: the month's dues generate for every student, each parent gets the message in your name, and the board turns green as the money lands.

Questions tutors ask

Is this free?

Yes, and there is no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Does it send the message for me?

No. It writes the message and copies it, or opens WhatsApp with the text already filled in. You press send.

Can I use these messages commercially?

Yes. Copy them, change them, make them yours. They are templates, not content we are protecting.

How do I send this to fourteen parents without doing it fourteen times?

That is what FeeSaathi does. It generates the month's dues for every student, writes each message in your name with a payment link, and sends them together. Free for the first 10 students.
