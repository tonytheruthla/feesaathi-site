Straight answers

Everything tutors ask me on the first call.

?Parents ko app download karna padega?

Nahi. Sab kuch WhatsApp par jaata hai, kisi aur message ki tarah. Parents install nothing and sign up for nothing.

?Paisa kiske account mein jaata hai?

Seedha aapke. Your own UPI ID or Razorpay account. FeeSaathi never holds your money and never takes a cut of a fee.

?I have 40 students, not 10.

Add all 40. The first 10 stay free forever, and I will move your Excel register across myself on a 10-minute call.

?Mera English theek nahi hai.

Messages go out in Hinglish or English, your choice. The app is built for tutors who teach in Hindi.

?Will parents find a reminder rude?

It reads like you on a good day: short, polite, the amount and a link. Tutors tell me the awkward phone call simply stopped happening.

?Who is behind this?

One person. I am Rahul, I built FeeSaathi after watching tutors chase fees on WhatsApp every month. Write to rahul@feesaathi.com and I answer myself.
